About Typowizz

Typowizz is a small, fast website built for one thing: helping you type better. You can take a classic typing speed test with real paragraphs and essays, see your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy live, and then train with arcade-style typing games.

There are no accounts, no passwords and no ads. Open the page and start typing. Your best scores are stored only on your own device, and the whole project is open source so anyone can read it, improve it or make their own copy.
